## The Format of This Blog and how to Navigate

I write this blog through Obsidian and I'm trying out an unconventional format for blogs and technical articles but I believe it is the best way for you to find and read what you are interested in and for me to find writing new entries effortless and can share more with the community.

> [!question] Why are some links broken and do not do anything when I click them?
> The format of this blog is in the style of Wiki-links so you can navigate back and forth from an entry to another looking for the pieces of information that might interest you be useful to you. Some Wiki-links will be disabled, what this means is that I plan to write an entry sometime in the future but haven't gotten to do it yet. Once it's written the link will appear light up and be usable. Good way to keep me accountable too.
